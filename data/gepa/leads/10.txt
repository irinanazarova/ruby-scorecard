Since my first year at Evil Martians, I’ve worked on many Rails applications: monoliths and moduliths (modular monoliths), API-only and Hotwired. All of them shared one common trait: test suites that left much to be improved in terms of speed. I started collecting tips and tricks on making tests faster, and eventually bundled them all into a meta-gem called TestProf, the Ruby test profiling toolbox. Before jumping into the “hows” of tests profiling and optimization, let’s talk a bit about “whys”. The motivation Slow tests waste your time by making you less productive. You’re probably asking yourself: “Why does a test suite’s performance matter?” Let me show you some statistics before proposing any arguments about that.
