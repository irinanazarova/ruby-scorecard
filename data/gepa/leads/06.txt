Your TS/JS application doesn't want to sit still: it needs WebSockets for all sorts of real-time messaging, streaming from LLMs, and collaborative features. What to choose? We benchmarked Socket.io, uWebSockets, and AnyCable across three rubrics: latency + throughput, reliability, and scalability. If we want to dance, let's dance smoothly. Every option runs as a standalone WS service on the same Railway box, apples-to-apples. Reproducible from the open-source bench repo. What we compare# Five production-shaped options, same hardware: Default Socket.io — baseline popular option. Socket.io + CSR — Socket.io with Connection state recovery, the opt-in for delivery and order guarantees. uWebSockets.js + topics — the “just use uWS” alternative, using its built-in subscribe/publish API. AnyCable OSS — a separate Go binary your app broadcasts to over HTTP, broker built in. AnyCable Pro — same protocol as OSS; denser per-connection memory, shared replay state, commercial license.
